What do Vinted's S/M/L sizes actually mean?

Vinted swapped UK numeric sizes for letter buckets, so ticking “14” now hands you 12s. Here is what the letters map to, which boxes to tick so you stop missing things, and what to write when you sell.

Based on Vinted's documented Nov 2025 sizing change for UK womenswear. Vinted has been changing this system through 2026 — accounts may see different layouts.

The tick-adjacent-buckets and check-measurements advice holds either way.

What changed, and when
  • 10 Nov 2025 — the changeUK womenswear sizes were collapsed into letter buckets with ranges (“S / UK 8–10”), and existing listings were auto-converted. Widely reported by users; this is the system this page encodes.
  • ~Jan 2026 — a partial reversion, unconfirmedSome users reported UK numeric sizes coming back. We could not confirm it landed, or for whom. Treat it as “some people may see numbers again”.
  • ~Jul 2026 — a per-account sizing picker, rolling outReports of a per-account “sizing standard” setting. If that is on your account, two people can be looking at the same listing and see different size labels. Another reason to write the number in the description.

Whatever the label says, check the measurements

This is the part that actually works, on any version of Vinted's UI. Letters and numbers are both the seller's guess; a tape measure is not.

  • Measure a garment you already own and love the fit of. That is your reference, not a size label.
  • Ask the seller for pit to pit (armpit to armpit, laid flat), shoulder to hem, and waist laid flat for anything fitted.
  • Ask for a photo of the size tag. It settles what the garment actually is, whatever bucket the listing sits in.
  • Remember laid-flat measurements are half the body measurement — a 52cm pit to pit is a 104cm chest.
  • If a seller won't measure, that is information too. Plenty of others will.

What this table does not cover, and why

  • No EU or US column. Vinted-specific EU/US labels for these buckets could not be verified, and generic conversions between the three systems are disputed. A wrong column would be worse than no column.
  • Womenswear tops, dresses and outerwear only. Jeans, trousers and shoes appear to have kept their numeric sizing — that is inferred from the absence of complaints about them, not from anything Vinted published.
  • No menswear, no kids'. Not verified, so not shown.
  • No sizes above XXL or below XS. We saw claims of further buckets but could not corroborate them, so they are not here.
  • No per-country variation. This is the UK womenswear view. If you are shopping another country's Vinted, assume none of the above holds.
