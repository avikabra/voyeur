Silhouette Try-On

A stylized preview of how a top might sit on you — built from your own photo and a photo of the garment. Not a photo. Nothing you upload ever leaves this device.

This tool needs JavaScript to detect your pose and draw the preview — there's no version that works without it, sorry. It still never sends anything anywhere once loaded.

What this actually is: a rough, honest fit sketch — your photo's pose, the garment's shape, warped together on a canvas. It cannot show fabric drape, folds, or how the material actually behaves. Think "sizing sketch," not "photo of you wearing it."

What never happens: your photos are read into the browser's memory and drawn to a canvas — that's it. No upload, no account, no server. Load this page with your network off after the first visit and it still works.

What this can't do
  • Not photorealistic. It cannot simulate fabric, drape, folds, stretch, or how a material actually moves — it's a warped 2D shape, honestly labeled as one.
  • Tops and dresses only, front-facing poses only. Trousers, jackets with structure, and side/angled photos aren't modeled.
  • Pose detection can miss or misjudge a pose in poor light, baggy clothing, unusual angles, or with more than one person in frame — you'll see a clear message rather than a warped guess.
  • Garment background removal is a plain-background heuristic (it samples the four corners of the photo). Busy or textured backgrounds may not cut out cleanly — the length/width sliders exist partly to compensate.
  • Runs a large (~18MB) pose-detection model the first time you use it, downloaded once and cached by your browser after.
  • Not affiliated with any brand or retailer.
