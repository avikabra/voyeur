Silhouette Try-On

See roughly how a garment would sit on you, before you buy it.

Both photos stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — every step runs in your browser. Turn your network off after this page has loaded once and it still works.

This is a rough schematic overlay, not a photorealistic try-on. It shows roughly where a garment would sit and roughly how wide it is against your shoulders and hips — not how the fabric will drape, stretch, or actually look.

1. A photo of you

Front-facing, head to hips visible, plain-ish pose. A normal selfie works.

2. A photo of the garment

A product photo on a plain background works best — a screenshot from a shop is fine.

Result

Limitations
  • Not photorealistic. It cannot show fabric drape, stretch, texture, or how a garment actually looks worn — only its rough position and width against your torso.
  • Only approximates the torso region (shoulders to hips). Sleeve, neckline, and hem detail are not modeled.
  • Needs a clear front-facing photo with your shoulders and hips visible. Side-on poses, heavy cropping, or poor lighting will fail to detect a pose.
  • Garment cutout works by removing a plain background — it will not cleanly separate a garment shot against a busy or textured background, on a model, or on a hanger with visible clutter.
  • The first load downloads about 15 MB of pose-detection model files (cached after that by your browser).
